# Potpuri: product guide

Potpuri is a web reading app whose mission is to make quality literature a daily habit. Its official website is https://potpuri.cc/. Potpuri presents books as short reading cards rather than one long scrolling document.

## Are the books free?

Yes. Every book in the current catalogue is free to read. The current catalogue contains public-domain literature. Paid AI assistance is a separate feature, not a requirement for reading those books. Public-domain status can vary by country.

## Can I try Potpuri without an account?

Yes. The homepage links to ten-card previews of:

- [A Christmas Carol by Charles Dickens](https://potpuri.cc/try/?book=gutenberg-46)
- [Beyond Good and Evil by Friedrich Nietzsche](https://potpuri.cc/try/?book=gutenberg-4363)
- [The Yellow Wallpaper by Charlotte Perkins Gilman](https://potpuri.cc/try/?book=the-yellow-wallpaper)
- [A Connecticut Yankee in King Arthur's Court by Mark Twain](https://potpuri.cc/try/?book=gutenberg-86)

The title card has no coaching bubble. Puri demonstrates word explanation on the first non-title card and a prepared French translation on the second non-title card. The remaining preview cards have no coaching bubbles. After ten cards, the preview offers account creation.

These demonstrations use prepared content and do not call a live AI service. Guest previews do not save account reading history or earn reading credits.

## What does an account provide?

An account saves your place and gives access to personal reading progress, streaks, highlights, quote sharing, book ratings, and friends. The Library offers All and My books views. My books contains books you have opened.

Potpuri currently requires users to be at least 13 years old. Signup asks for an email address, handle, password, date of birth, and time zone. Optional authenticator-based two-factor authentication has recovery codes. Email confirmation and password reset are supported when transactional email delivery is configured. Unverified accounts can still read; the profile offers a confirmation-email resend link.

## How do streaks work?

A qualifying day requires at least three credited text cards, using the reader's own time zone. Title and chapter-heading cards do not earn reading credits. Merely opening a book or saving a position does not earn credits.

## How do friends and common books work?

Friendships require mutual acceptance. The friends list shows nickname, handle, most recent visible book, and the number of books in common. Opening a friend's profile shows their permitted reading details.

Books in common are distinct canonical books both readers have started or earned real reading credits for, including different editions of the same book. Starting a book can contribute to this comparison without granting streak or page credits. A friend's private books or private profile do not contribute. Profiles default to friends-only visibility; users can change profile visibility and hide individual books.

## What is Puri?

Puri is Potpuri's reading companion. Readers can ask about selected words or phrases, or translate a reading card into French, Spanish, or German. Plus/Pro retain included AI; membership card translations may be saved and reused.

Free BYOK — for the brave — supports user-owned OpenAI Platform, Anthropic and OpenRouter API keys with 50 app-enforced requests per account per UTC calendar month. Your provider bills your API usage. Requests go directly from the browser to that provider; keys and BYOK translations stay in page memory and are cleared by reload/navigation. Failed/cancelled attempts count. This allowance is not a provider spending cap. ChatGPT/Codex subscription credentials are not accepted as API keys.

The membership AI service uses OpenRouter and Google Gemini. AI answers may be inaccurate. The current [privacy page](https://potpuri.cc/legal/privacy) describes what is sent to providers. Check the live app for current membership availability, pricing, and usage limits.
